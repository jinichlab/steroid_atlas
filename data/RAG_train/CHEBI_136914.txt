== ChEBI definition ==
A steroid glucuronide anion that is the conjugate base of 5α-dihydrotestosterone 17-<em>O</em>-(β-<small>D</small>-glucuronide) arising from deprotonation of the carboxylic acid function; major species at pH 7.3.
