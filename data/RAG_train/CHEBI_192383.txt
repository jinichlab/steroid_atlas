== ChEBI definition ==
3-oxo-chol-4-ene-(22R)-hydroxy-24-oyl-CoA(4-) is a steroidal acyl-CoA(4-). It is functionally related to a (22E)-3-oxochola-4,22-dien-24-oyl-CoA(4-).
