== ChEBI definition ==
11beta-hydroxyandrost-4-ene-3,17-dione is a 17-oxo steroid, an 11beta-hydroxy steroid, a 3-oxo steroid and an androstanoid. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and a human metabolite.
