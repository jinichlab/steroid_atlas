== ChEBI definition ==
A  steroid saponin that is (3β,22<i>E</i>)-stigmasta-5,22-dien-3-ol attached to a β-<small>D</small>-glucopyranosyl residue at position 3 via a glycosidic linkage. It is isolated from   <em>Symplocos lancifolia</em>.
