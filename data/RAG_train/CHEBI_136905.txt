== ChEBI definition ==
A steroid glucuronide anion that is the conjugate base of hyodeoxycholic acid 6-<em>O</em>-(β-<small>D</small>-glucuronide) arising from deprotonation of the carboxylic acid functions; major species at pH 7.3.
