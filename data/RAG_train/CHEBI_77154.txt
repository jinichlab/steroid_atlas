== ChEBI definition ==
A ginsenoside found in <em>Panax ginseng</em> that is dammarane which is substituted by hydroxy groups at the 3β, 12β and 20 <em>pro</em>-<i>S</i> positions, in which the hydroxy groups at positions 3 and 20 have been converted to the corresponding β-<small>D</small>-glucopyranosyl-(1→2)-β-<small>D</small>-glucopyranoside and α-<small>L</small>-arabinofuranosyl-(1→6)-β-<small>D</small>-glucopyranoside respectively, and in which a double bond has been introduced at the 24-25 position.
