== ChEBI definition ==
A 3β-hydroxy-Δ<small><sup>5</small></sup>-steroid that is desmosterol in which the double bond at position 24-25 has been oxidised to the corresponding epoxide (the 24<i>S</i> diastereoisomer). It is an oxysterol agonist of the liver X receptor.
