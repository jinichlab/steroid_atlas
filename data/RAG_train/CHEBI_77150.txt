== ChEBI definition ==
A ginsenoside found in <em>Panax</em> species that is dammarane which is substituted by hydroxy groups at the 3β, 6α, 12β and 20 <em>pro</em>-<i>S</i> positions, in which the hydroxy group at position 20 has been converted to the corresponding β-<small>D</small>-glucopyranoside, and in which a double bond has been introduced at the 24-25 position.
