== ChEBI definition ==
7alpha,12alpha-dihydroxycholest-4-en-3-one is a 7alpha-hydroxy steroid, a 12alpha-hydroxy steroid, a 3-oxo-Delta(4) steroid and a cholestanoid. It has a role as a mouse metabolite and a human metabolite.
