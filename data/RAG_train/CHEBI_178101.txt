== ChEBI definition ==
A steroid glucuronide anion that is the conjugate base of estrone 3-<em>O</em>-(3-sulfo-β-<small>D</small>-glucuronide) arising from deprotonation of the carboxylic and sulfate groups; major species at pH 7.3.
