== ChEBI definition ==
16alpha-hydroxyandrost-4-ene-3,17-dione is a 16alpha-hydroxy steroid, a 17-oxo steroid, a secondary alpha-hydroxy ketone, a 3-oxo steroid and an androstanoid. It has a role as a mouse metabolite.
