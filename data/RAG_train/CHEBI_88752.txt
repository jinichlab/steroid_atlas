== ChEBI definition ==
A cholesteryl octadecatrienoate obtained by formal condensation of the carboxy group of (5<i>Z</i>,8<i>Z</i>,11<i>Z</i>)-icosatrienoic acid with the hydroxy group of cholesterol.
