== ChEBI definition ==
A 3-oxo-5α-steroid that is 5α-estrane which is substituted by an oxo group at position 3, a hydroxy group at the 17β position, and which has a double bond between positions 1 and 10. It is a 19-norsteroid arising from successive oxidation of dihydrotestosterone at <em>C</em><small><sub>19</sub></small>.
